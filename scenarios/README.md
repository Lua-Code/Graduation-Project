Store version-controlled synthetic scenario definitions here. Keep generated run output elsewhere.

A scenario's map is loaded from `maps/<mapId with - replaced by _>.json`.

## Maps

| File | Map ID | Size | Roads | Facilities |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `maps/synthetic_city_01.json` | `synthetic-city-01` | 2 km × 2 km, 6 nodes | 7 roads, 800 m | 1 hospital, 1 fire station |
| `maps/synthetic_city_02.json` | `synthetic-city-02` | 3 km × 3 km, 6 × 6 grid (36 nodes) | 60 roads, 500 m | 2 hospitals, 2 fire stations, 1 rescue centre |

`synthetic-city-02` node IDs are `n<row><col>` (`n00` top-left, `n55` bottom-right). Roads
are `h<row><col>` (east to the next column) and `v<row><col>` (south to the next row).
Rows 1 and 4 and columns 1 and 4 are arterials at 16.7 m/s (3 ticks per road); the rest
are local roads at 11.1 m/s (5 ticks). Hospitals are at `n11` and `n44`.

## Scenarios

| File | Map | Magnitude | Epicentre | Degraded multiplier | Use |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `earthquake_small.json` | city-01 | 6.5 | (1000, 1000) | 2.0 | Smoke tests, fixtures |
| `earthquake_city02_moderate.json` | city-02 | 5.5 | centre | 2.0 | Light damage |
| `earthquake_city02_severe.json` | city-02 | 7.5 | centre | 2.0 | Heavy damage near both hospitals |
| `earthquake_city02_offcentre.json` | city-02 | 7.0 | (500, 2500), south-west | 2.0 | Damage on one side of the city |
| `earthquake_city02_severe_slow_roads.json` | city-02 | 7.5 | centre | 3.0 | Sensitivity to the multiplier (ADR-0002) |

All `city02` scenarios share the same seed (101), 10 units and 8 incidents (18 victims).
Comparing two of them therefore changes only the earthquake setup. To repeat a scenario
with other seeds, change `seed` in code (for example `scenario.model_copy(update={"seed": s})`)
rather than adding a file per seed.

## Adding or changing scenarios

- `synthetic-city-02` and its scenarios are generated by `scripts/generate_city02.py`.
  Edit the script and rerun it (`python scripts/generate_city02.py`); a test fails if the
  files and the script disagree.
- Every map and scenario in this folder is checked by `tests/unit/test_scenario_catalog.py`
  using `drsim.simulation.world.validation`: roads are at least as long as the straight line
  between their nodes, every node can reach every other node, the map has a hospital, IDs
  are unique, and every incident can be reached by a unit and can reach a hospital.
